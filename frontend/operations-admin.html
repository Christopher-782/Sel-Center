Profit Formula

How the figures are calculated.

Gross Profit = Total Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold

Net Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses

Kitchen product cost is stored on each Kitchen sale at the time it is completed. Tickets and Gate Entry have zero COGS, so their full selling value contributes to gross profit before expenses.
