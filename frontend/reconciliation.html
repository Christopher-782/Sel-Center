How Cash Change Works

Example of a customer collecting change.

Sale total: ₦4,500

Customer gives: ₦5,000

Change returned: ₦500

Drawer increase: ₦4,500

The POS stores both ₦5,000 received and ₦500 change. Reconciliation therefore expects only ₦4,500 from that transaction.
